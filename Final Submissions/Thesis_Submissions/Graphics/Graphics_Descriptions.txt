Graphics Descriptions for Project Uber Thesis

=======================================================

week2_daily_rides.png
Description: Daily total ride counts during the observation period.
Significance: Establishes the baseline pattern of daily ride volume and confirms the cyclical nature of weekday versus weekend activity.

-------

week2_daily_rides_7day.png
Description: Seven-day moving average of daily ride counts.
Significance: Smooths short-term fluctuations to highlight weekly mobility cycles and recurring demand peaks.

-------

week2_daily_rides_28day.png
Description: Twenty-eight-day moving average of ride volume trends.
Significance: Reveals long-term demand momentum and helps identify seasonality or persistent shifts in ride-hailing activity.

-------

week2_corr_matrix.png
Description: Correlation matrix of trip-level features such as fare, distance, duration, and surge multiplier.
Significance: Aids in feature selection by identifying highly correlated variables that may affect model interpretability or performance.

-------

Significance: Illustrates the skewed nature of mobility data, supporting log-transformations applied later in the modeling process.
