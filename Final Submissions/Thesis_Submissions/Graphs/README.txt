Project Title:
Machine Learning Framework for Ride-Hailing Demand, Revenue, 
and Operational Analysis in Delhi NCR

==============================================================
1. Overview
==============================================================
This folder contains all graphs and visualizations generated 
throughout the thesis. Each figure corresponds to a section 
within the final paper and supports the analysis of ride-hailing 
operations, predictive modeling, and sustainability outcomes.

All figures are labeled clearly and include descriptive captions 
to explain their analytical significance. These visuals were 
produced either from Python scripts in the `Code` folder or 
from visualization tools such as Draw.io and Matplotlib.

==============================================================
2. Figures and Descriptions
==============================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------
Figure 1 – Problem Context Map
--------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Illustrates the demand–supply imbalance cycle in ride-hailing 
systems, showing how high demand and driver shortages lead to 
increased cancellations and reduced long-term reliability.  
Significance:
Establishes the motivation for applying machine learning to 
forecasting and operational optimization.

--------------------------------------------------------------
Figure 2 – Project Workflow Overview
--------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Outlines the multi-stage analytical pipeline used in the project, 
from data acquisition and preprocessing to model training and 
evaluation.  
Significance:
Demonstrates the iterative, reproducible process adopted during 
the eight-week implementation period.

--------------------------------------------------------------
Figure 3 – Model Performance Comparison
--------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Compares baseline and ensemble model performance (Linear Regression, 
Random Forest, XGBoost) across accuracy metrics.  
Significance:
Highlights improvements achieved through ensemble learning methods 
for fare and demand prediction.

--------------------------------------------------------------
Figure 4 – Dynamic Pricing System Architecture
--------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Depicts the system-level architecture for real-time pricing and 
dispatch optimization in ride-hailing platforms.  
Significance:
Shows how predictive components interact with operational layers 
to inform surge pricing and resource allocation.

--------------------------------------------------------------
Figure 5 – Fairness, Sustainability, and Governance Framework
--------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Visual representation of the fairness-aware optimization and 
sustainability evaluation components integrated into the model.  
Significance:
Emphasizes the social and environmental alignment of model outcomes.

--------------------------------------------------------------
Figure 6 – Conceptual Framework
--------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Outlines the theoretical model linking data-driven operations, 
predictive analytics, and fairness objectives.  
Significance:
Provides a conceptual foundation connecting urban mobility research 
and applied machine learning.

--------------------------------------------------------------
Figure 7 – Data Preprocessing Workflow
--------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Shows the cleaning, transformation, and formatting stages applied 
to raw ride data before feature engineering.  
Significance:
Ensures reproducibility by visualizing how raw data becomes 
structured input for predictive models.

--------------------------------------------------------------
Figure 8 – Feature Engineering Pipeline
--------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Visual summary of feature derivation steps, including temporal, 
spatial, and behavioral attributes.  
Significance:
Illustrates the process of creating model-ready variables critical 
to accurate forecasting.

--------------------------------------------------------------
Figure 9 – Model Training and Evaluation Pipeline
--------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Presents the machine learning workflow for model fitting, 
cross-validation, and evaluation of performance metrics.  
Significance:
Documents the systematic experimentation framework applied to 
compare model accuracy.

--------------------------------------------------------------
Figure 10 – Combined Performance and Feature Importance
--------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Integrates visual summaries of tuned ensemble models, including 
Random Forest and XGBoost, with SHAP and feature importance plots.  
Significance:
Demonstrates interpretability and comparative performance of 
advanced predictive models.

--------------------------------------------------------------
Figure 11 – Hourly and Weekly Demand Heatmap
--------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Heatmap illustrating ride-hailing demand patterns by hour of day 
and day of week.  
Significance:
Reveals temporal peaks that guide fleet allocation and staffing 
strategies.

--------------------------------------------------------------
Figure 12 – Fare Prediction Comparison
--------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Compares predicted vs. actual fare outcomes using advanced models, 
highlighting residuals and variance reduction.  
Significance:
Validates model accuracy and demonstrates improvements over 
baseline predictions.

--------------------------------------------------------------
Figure 13 – Surge Distribution Heatmap
--------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Visualizes geographic or temporal variation in surge pricing 
intensity across different times and zones.  
Significance:
Provides insight into fairness and pricing consistency in the 
ride-hailing ecosystem.

--------------------------------------------------------------
Figure 14 – Demand Fulfillment and Cancellation
--------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Shows comparative analysis of successful rides, cancellations, 
and “No Driver Found” cases across time windows.  
Significance:
Highlights operational bottlenecks and platform responsiveness 
under different demand conditions.

--------------------------------------------------------------
Figure 15 – Regional Ride Flow / Zone Matrix
--------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Depicts inter-zone ride flows within the Delhi NCR area, showing 
trip densities and spatial imbalance patterns.  
Significance:
Supports fairness analysis by visualizing the spatial distribution 
of ride allocations.

--------------------------------------------------------------
Figure 16 – Emission Reduction Visualization
--------------------------------------------------------------
Description:
Illustrates the reduction in idle vehicle kilometers and 
fleet-level emissions achieved under optimized dispatch 
conditions.  
Significance:
Links operational efficiency improvements to measurable 
sustainability outcomes.

==============================================================
3. Folder Structure
==============================================================
All graphs are stored in PNG format with standardized naming:
    Figure1_Problem_Context_Map.png
    Figure2_Project_Workflow_Overview.png
    ...
    Figure16_Emission_Reduction.png

Each file name corresponds to its citation in the thesis paper 
and section heading (Figures 1–16).
